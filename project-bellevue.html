Alistair Drummond Architect

Bellevue Road, Oranjezicht

About

The complex forms part of a 3 storied 3 unit development in a strong Urban Conservation area. Gabled roofs were maintained to comply with the contemporary styling in the area. The upper levels use glass in order to capture the spectacular mountain views in both directions.

The overall design again was to scale the building down by defragmenting the different roofs as seen. This provides each unit with a sense of identity.

The project is rich in choice of materials. Although there is high use of steel & glass in the upper levels, there is the use of natural stone & tiles, cladded timber etc. mixed in a way to create a certain interest as seen in the combination of materials used.
